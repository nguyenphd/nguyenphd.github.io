Duy P. Nguyen

M.Sc. Global Environmental Economics, University of Galway (Ireland),
B.Sc. Natural Resource Economics, Can Tho University (Vietnam).

I am an environmental economist interested in applying my economic expertise to answer interdisciplinary questions about climate change, non-market values, behavioral changes, and the SDGs. My recent study was conducted under the UN Climate Change Programme at UG, where I investigated how local economic development influences adaptation to climate-related public health risks in Colombia, using nationally representative household survey, climate, and spatial data.

Research interests: Econometrics · Environmental Economic Modelling · Public Preferences · Social Changes.
