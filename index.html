Duy Nguyen (Jay)

I am an M.Sc. student in Global Environmental Economics at the University of Galway, Ireland.

Previously, I worked as a researcher and consultant specialising in environmental and behavioural economics in Vietnam. My work examines how environmental change, economic development, institutions, and human behaviour shape public health, food systems, climate resilience, and sustainable urban transitions.

I am open to research collaboration, doctoral opportunities, and consulting projects in environmental economics, applied behavioural economics and sustainable development.

Climate Variability, Local Development, and Household Hospitalisation in Colombia

Duy P. Nguyen

M.Sc. placement research, 2026

A household-level empirical study combining Colombia DHS surveys, ERA5-Land climate data, and a pre-shock Human Footprint measure to examine heterogeneous health effects across more- and less-developed departments.

Food Loss and the Low-Value Trap in Vietnam's Pangasius Value Chain

Duy P. Nguyen and collaborators

Systems thinking and causal-loop analysis

This research uses causal-loop diagrams to explain how survival-driven cost minimisation, price volatility, delayed investment, and volume-based competition reproduce inefficiency and food loss across the value chain.
