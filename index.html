Duy Nguyen (Jay)

I completed the M.Sc. in Global Environmental Economics at the University of Galway, Ireland. My M.Sc. thesis was conducted under the UN Climate Change Universities Partnership Programme (Nairobi Work Programme), where I investigated how local economic development influences adaptation to climate-related public health risks in Colombia, using nationally representative household survey and climate data.

Previously, I worked as a researcher and consultant specialising in environmental and behavioural economics in Vietnam. My work examines how environmental change, economic development, institutions, and human behaviour shape public health, food systems, climate resilience, and sustainable urban transitions.

I am open to job and doctoral opportunities, research collaboration and consulting projects in applied environmental and behavioural economics, social change and sustainable development.
